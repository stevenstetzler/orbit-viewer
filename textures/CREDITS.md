# Credits and license information

Licensing and citation details for the images in this folder.

## What the USGS Astropedia pages and API provide

Astropedia has no machine-readable license field (no SPDX identifier or license
URL). Each product page does carry a few free-text fields, copied below:

- **Access Constraints**: 'public domain' on 11, 'none' on 6 of the 17 USGS products.
- **Use Constraints**: 'please cite authors' on 11, 'none' on 6.
- Authors, originators and publisher, the source data archive, and a list of
  references written into the abstract/process description.

"Public domain" is stated explicitly on the products marked so. For the rest,
"None" means the page states no restriction, and "Please cite authors" asks for
attribution. Nothing on these pages adds restrictions beyond that. The USGS
STAC API (`stac.astrogeology.usgs.gov`) has no collection covering these
global maps, so it offers nothing further. These are the pages' own words, not
legal advice; check a product page before redistributing anything.

## Earth and Moon (NASA)

- `earth.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory, *Blue Marble Next Generation*, September
  2004 (5400x2700), downloaded from the NASA asset URL in `README.md`. I did not
  re-check the credit line or usage terms for this image from NASA's own page;
  see the Blue Marble collection (https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble)
  for those.

  The SVS page asks: "Please give credit for this item to: NASA's Scientific
  Visualization Studio", with visualizer Ernie Wright (STC) and scientist Noah
